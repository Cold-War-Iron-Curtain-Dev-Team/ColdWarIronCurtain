# Uncommunicated focus progression audit (2026-11-25)

Scope: the 545 focus files loaded by the game (top level of `common/national_focus/`; subfolders are not loaded), plus the events, scripted effects and decisions they reach. Paths are relative to `Cold War Iron Curtain/`.

Triggered by a Discord question: "what do I do after *Invite US Senator McCarthy to Visit Germany*?" That focus schedules Schumacher's stroke 15 days later inside `hidden_effect`, and the next branch is hidden behind `allow_branch`, so the tree looks finished. It has been fixed with a tooltip (`WGR_Schumacher_Health_Warning_tt`).

## Summary

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focuses whose continuation is hidden or delayed with no hint to the player (the McCarthy pattern) | 22 (5 high) | Each checked by a verifier and a skeptic agent |
| 2 | Date windows that can never be true (upper bound earlier than lower bound) | 10 (+12 same-day windows) | Mechanical |

## 1. Hidden / delayed continuation with no hint (verified)

Severity: **high** means the tree visibly dead-ends and the wait is long, random or permanent; **medium** means a real but shorter or partly hinted wait; **low** is minor. Severity is the skeptic's corrected value.

## Method and what was ruled out

It followed each focus's completion effects through events, `hidden_effect`, `immediate`, scripted effects and flag-triggered MTTH events to anything that unlocks, completes or reveals focuses in the same tree. It flagged 73 focuses where the path includes a hidden step with a delay. The original McCarthy case was used as a positive control, and the analyser detects it.
2. One agent per batch checked each candidate against the real files, and two more agents swept for patterns the analyser cannot see (daily-pulse date triggers, variables, cross-country replies, decisions). A separate skeptic agent then tried to refute every claimed finding.
