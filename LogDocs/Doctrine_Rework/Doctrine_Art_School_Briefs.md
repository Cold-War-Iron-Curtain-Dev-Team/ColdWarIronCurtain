# Doctrine Art: School Search Briefs

Companion to `Doctrine_Art_Sourcing_Plan.md` and `Doctrine_Art_Manifest.csv`. One brief per land
school: who, what they fielded, which events are photographed, and where to look. Track rows say what
each of the four track backgrounds should show.

## Forward Defense (NATO)

- Countries: USA, UK, West Germany, Canada, Netherlands, Belgium, Italy, Turkey; SADF lineage.
- Signature kit: M48/M60/M1 Abrams, Leopard 1/2, Chieftain, M113, M109, M110, AH-1/AH-64, TOW, MLRS,
  Pershing; Bundeswehr Jaeger and Panzergrenadier.
- Photographed events: REFORGER exercises (1969-1993), Fulda Gap border patrols, BAOR exercises
  (Lionheart 1984), Bundeswehr maneuvers, Berlin Brigade.
- Tracks: infantry = US/BW mechanized infantry dismounting from M113/Marder; combat support = M109 or
  MLRS firing, AH-1 Cobra; armor = M60 or Leopard columns on REFORGER; operations = NATO HQ map room,
  convoy on autobahn.
- Medallion: M1 Abrams or Leopard at the inner-German border.
- Sources: DVIDS, NARA (DoD photos are PD), Commons "REFORGER", "Bundeswehr 1980s", Bundesarchiv
  B 145 series (CC BY-SA 3.0 DE).

## Deep Battle (Soviet / Warsaw Pact)

- Countries: USSR, East Germany, Poland, Czechoslovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria; DPRK lineage.
- Signature kit: T-54/55, T-62, T-72, BMP-1/2, BTR-60/70, 2S1/2S3, BM-21 Grad, Scud, Mi-24.
- Photographed events: Zapad-81, Shield exercises, Prague 1968 (Soviet tanks), NVA parades and
  maneuvers, Soviet-Afghan War.
- Tracks: infantry = motor rifle troops dismounting BMP; combat support = BM-21 salvo or 2S3 battery
  (replaces the tunnel); armor = T-72 column (keep current T-55 if user approves); operations =
  Soviet staff at map table, tank column on a march.
- Medallion: T-72 column or Red Army parade.
- Sources: Bundesarchiv Bild 183 (NVA, CC BY-SA), Commons "Soviet Army in the 1980s",
  RIA Novosti archive on Commons (CC BY-SA 3.0), NARA (US intelligence photos of Soviet forces).

## People's War (PRC / North Vietnam / Maoist)

- Countries: PRC, North Vietnam, Khmer Rouge, Maoist movements.
- Signature kit: Type 56 rifle, Type 59 tank, Type 63 MLRS, militia formations, bicycle and porter
  logistics.
- Photographed events: Korean War PVA, Sino-Vietnamese War 1979, Ho Chi Minh Trail, Dien Bien Phu,
  PLA drills.
- Tracks: infantry = PLA or PAVN infantry; combat support = porter columns or Type 63 rockets;
  armor = Type 59 / T-54 of PAVN (Saigon 1975 tank 843 is iconic); operations = commanders at map
  (Giap at Dien Bien Phu).
- Medallion: PLA or PAVN soldier with flag.
- Sources: Commons "People's Liberation Army", "Vietnam People's Army", NARA captured-document photos,
  Vietnam News Agency items released to Commons.

## Intervention and Pacification (France / colonial)

- Countries: France, Portugal, Belgium.
- Signature kit: AMX-13, EBR and Panhard AML, Alouette helicopters, paratroopers, Foreign Legion.
- Photographed events: Indochina and Algeria, Suez 1956, Kolwezi 1978, Chad (Operation Manta),
  Portuguese colonial wars.
- Tracks: infantry = paras or Legion patrol; combat support = Alouette/H-21 helicopter landing;
  armor = AMX-13 or EBR in Algeria; operations = paras on airfield before drop or HQ.
- Medallion: French paratrooper in beret.
- Sources: Commons "Algerian War", "First Indochina War", "Portuguese Colonial War"; ECPAD only where
  Commons hosts a free release.

## Preemptive Maneuver (Israel)

- Signature kit: Centurion (Sho't), M48/M60 (Magach), Merkava, M113 (Zelda), paratroopers.
- Photographed events: Six-Day War, Yom Kippur War, Lebanon 1982.
- Tracks: infantry = paratroopers at the Western Wall or Golani; combat support = M109/artillery in
  Sinai; armor = Centurion column in Sinai/Golan; operations = commanders over maps (Dayan, Sharon).
- Medallion: tank commander in Sinai.
- Sources: GPO National Photo Collection (commons-released), IDF Flickr (CC BY-SA/CC BY-NC check),
  Commons "Six-Day War", "Yom Kippur War".

## Total National Defense

- Countries: Yugoslavia, Sweden, Switzerland, Finland, Austria, India (Himalayan), Iran.
- Signature kit: JNA M-84, Swedish Strv 103 and Ikv, Swiss reservists, Finnish ski troops, Indian
  mountain troops, Iranian infantry in the Iran-Iraq War.
- Tracks: infantry = Finnish ski troops or Swiss militia; combat support = mountain artillery or
  obstacle engineers; armor = Strv 103 or M-84; operations = JNA / Territorial Defense exercise.
- Medallion: reservist with rifle in mountains.
- Sources: SA-kuva (Finnish Defence Forces, CC BY 4.0), Commons "Yugoslav People's Army",
  "Swedish Army", Digitalt Museum (Sweden, many CC), Commons "Indian Army", "Iran-Iraq War".

## Revolutionary Insurgency

- Movements: Cuban revolutionaries, FRELIMO, MPLA, ZANLA, PAIGC, mujahideen, FSLN; Cuban
  internationalists in Angola.
- Signature kit: AK-47, RPG-7, technicals, captured armor (insurgent armor track should be captured
  or allied armor: Cuban T-55 in Angola, FSLN tanks, mujahideen on captured BMP).
- Tracks: infantry = guerrilla column; combat support = RPG / mortar team; armor = Cuban T-55 in
  Angola or mujahideen on captured vehicle (replaces the group of fighters); operations = Sierra
  Maestra leadership or camp briefing.
- Medallion: guerrilla with rifle and flag.
- Sources: Commons "Cuban Revolution", "Soviet-Afghan War", "Mozambican War of Independence",
  NARA / USAID photos of Afghanistan.
