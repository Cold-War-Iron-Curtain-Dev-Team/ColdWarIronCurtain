# Doctrine Icon Art Requests

64x64 doctrine placeholder. NATO is fully illustrated and Himalayan is partly done;
these are the gaps.

Drop a 64x64 PNG named `<technology>.png` into
`Cold War Iron Curtain/gfx/interface/doctrines/Technology/`, then repoint that
technology's `texturefile` in `interface/CWIC_Doctrines.gfx` from the placeholder to
`gfx/interface/doctrines/Technology/<technology>.png`.
