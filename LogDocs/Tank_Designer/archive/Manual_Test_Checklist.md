## 5a. Tech tree layout - FIXED, needs a visual re-check

The screenshots in `TankPictures/` showed overlapping tech boxes and techs sitting on the year ruler.
Cause: the earlier pass satisfied the validator's unique-coordinate contract by relocating 31 techs to
arbitrary "unused" cells, which ignored the tree's column semantics - it dumped engine and armor techs
into the mechanized column at x=0 and scattered `aiming_devices`, `optics` and `ballistic_calculator`
across four different columns each.

What changed: all 31 relocations were reverted, then the 11 genuine collisions were resolved by keeping
each family in one straight column. Year-anchored rows (`y = @1955` instead of `y = 6`) were kept, since
those align a tech with the year ruler and the validator asserts them.
