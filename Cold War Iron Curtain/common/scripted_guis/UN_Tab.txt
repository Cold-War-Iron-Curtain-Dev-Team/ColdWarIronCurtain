# the resolution dropdown, the target and state pickers and the submit button
# are gone, along with every write to un_action_set.

# Resolutions now only ARRIVE. The mechanism already existed and is untouched:
# UN_HISTORICAL_RESOLUTION_WEEKLY_CHECK in UN_Scripted_Effects.txt runs weekly
# on UNS and fires a resolution when its conditions are met. Content fires the
# rest. What is left in this file is the part that was always the interesting
# half - seeing the resolution, seeing who voted how, and voting.
#
# The player's lever on other countries is now a diplomatic action instead of a
# menu: opt_un_lobby_action in scripted_diplomatic_actions.txt.
