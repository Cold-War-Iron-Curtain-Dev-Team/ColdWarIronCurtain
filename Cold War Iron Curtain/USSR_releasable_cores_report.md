## Notes

- **RUS (Russian SFSR) has no cores at all.** Every other union republic has them. This is the biggest gap.
- Seven ASSRs exist as states in the rework but their tags core nothing: Bashkir, Chuvash, Komi, Mordovian, Mari, Tatar, Kalmyk.
- BSK, CHV, KOM, MRD, MRI, RMI and TTR are not referenced *anywhere* in the mod — no event, decision or focus mentions them.
- RMI "Romani ASSR" has no corresponding territory on the map.
- The remaining tags (ECR, IRF, RFE, RLG, RNC, RNU, LBR, USO, MRP, AZK) look like 1991-collapse / civil-war factions that would normally be given cores by event at the time of release.
